PROJECT\_DNA: HealthScan (The Literacy Engine)

1\. The Core Mission

The Aim: To democratize chemical literacy.



The Villain: Confusion, jargon, and information asymmetry on product labels.



The Hero Moment: The "Aha!" moment when a user understands a scientific term they previously found intimidating.



The Goal: "I feel educated."



2\. Scope \& Verticals

Consumables: Food, Beverages, and Supplements (Focus: Hidden sugars, additives, ultra-processing).



Topicals: Cosmetics and Personal Care (Focus: INCI nomenclature, allergens, endocrine disruptors).



Household: Cleaning supplies (Focus: Surfactants, synthetic fragrances, environmental impact).



3\. Product Principles (The "How")

Interpreter Logic: Never show a chemical name in isolation. Always pair it with its Common Name and its Function (e.g., "Sodium Benzoate — Preservative").



Educational Tone: Avoid "Traffic Light" colors (Red/Green). Use a neutral "Research" palette (Blues, Purples, Teals) to encourage objective learning over fear-based reactions.



Allergen Guardrails: An optional, user-defined toggle. If active, the app must identify not just the allergen, but the scientific aliases of that allergen (e.g., "Caseinate" for Milk).



4\. Technical Infrastructure

Mobile Stack: React Native + Expo (SDK 55+).



Vision Engine: Hybrid Model (Barcode-First via Open Food Facts API; Fallback to Google ML Kit OCR).



Intelligence: Gemini 2.5 Flash (Used for error correction, jargon translation, and regulatory context).



Data Standards: 2026 EFSA/FDA/ECHA compliance.



5\. The Team Roles

Innovation Lead: Strategy and "Education-First" feature design.



Toxicology \& Science Lead: Regulatory accuracy and chemical/INCI data.



UX \& Behavioral Architect: Cognitive clarity, Nudge theory, and UI/UX flow.



Technical Systems Architect: Systems design and lead supervisor for Claude Code.



Claude Code (External): The Developer responsible for implementation.
